# Security reports

Do not attach Home Assistant exports, credentials, recordings, private speaker or
phone identifiers, or signed recording URLs to issues. Share a minimal synthetic
reproduction and sanitized version information.

For a suspected vulnerability, use this repository's private vulnerability
reporting feature when available. Otherwise open an issue requesting a private
contact without disclosing exploit details or household data.

Local HA administrator access controls configuration. Keep Home Assistant updated,
restrict LAN access, and configure only trusted speech and media providers.
